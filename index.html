Offers

Our preferred rates reward guests who choose to extend their pleasure or plan ahead. We shape our offers as invitations to settle in and experience the house at its intended pace.

Extend Your Pleasure

Created for guests who choose to stay a little longer, Extend Your Pleasure offers preferred rates on stays of three nights or more. The offer invites a slower rhythm, encouraging time to settle into the suite, the rooftop, and the quiet rituals of being here. It reflects the hotel’s ethos of autonomy and immersion rather than fleeting visits.

Dining

Our rooftop dining unfolds within a secluded, garden-like setting where Mediterranean influences meet seasonal ingredients and shared plates. We create an atmosphere that feels intimate and immersive, offering elevated views without ever feeling exposed.

Weddings and Events

We host gatherings with a focus on privacy, flexibility, and a distinctly residential tone. From rooftop celebrations to focused leadership retreats, each event is designed to feel like a private world temporarily opened to invited guests.

Location

We are tucked into a quiet residential pocket just below the Sunset Strip, balancing access with seclusion. Guests remain moments from Los Angeles' most dynamic cultural corridors while feeling entirely removed from their performance.
